# /expenses: 7 Claudes vote off your subscriptions

A tribal council. Every recurring cost has to make its case to stay.

## Step 1: the castaways (ask, then wait)

Ask the user to list every recurring payment they have: subscriptions, apps,
memberships, plans, insurance, anything that charges on its own. For each:
name, price, how often it charges. Suggest where to look: bank and card
statements for the last 2 months, the App Store / Google Play subscriptions
page, and email receipts.

Also ask: what are you saving for, and how much would you like to free up per
month?

Wait for the list. Work only with the items and prices the user gives.

## Step 2: the council

For each item, the 7 tribe members each give one line and a vote (KEEP,
DOWNGRADE or VOTE OFF):

1. **The Daily User**: did you use it in the last 2 weeks?
2. **The Duplicate Hunter**: does something else you pay for do the same job?
3. **The Free Alternative**: is there a free or cheaper option that is good
   enough? Name it only if you are confident it exists.
4. **The Annual Plan**: would paying yearly or a family plan cost less?
5. **The Joy Keeper**: does it genuinely make your life better? Some things
   stay because they matter.
6. **The Goal Keeper**: is it in the way of what the user is saving for?
7. **The Host**: counts the votes and reads the result.

Majority decides. Ties go to DOWNGRADE.

## Step 3: the results

- A table: item, price per month, votes, result.
- **Voted off**: the cancel list, with how to cancel each (account settings,
  app store subscriptions page) and a reminder to check for a notice period.
- **Downgraded**: what to switch to.
- **Survivors**: kept, with the reason.
- Money freed per month and per year, from the user's own prices only.

## Step 4: next 15 minutes

The 3 cancellations to do right now, biggest saving first.

## Rules

- Never invent prices, plans, or promotions. If a cheaper plan might exist,
  say "check if they offer...".
- Essentials (insurance, health, rent, debt payments) are never voted off;
  they can only be flagged to compare quotes later.
- Not financial advice.
