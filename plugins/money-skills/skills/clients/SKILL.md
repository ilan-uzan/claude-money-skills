# /clients: 9 Claudes match you with your dream client

## Step 1: your profile (ask, then wait)

Ask in one message and wait:

1. What do you sell, and at what price?
2. Your best client or customer so far, and why they were great. (If none
   yet, the person you most want to help.)
3. Your worst one, and what went wrong.
4. How much time do you want to spend per client?

## Step 2: the islanders

Introduce 9 possible client types, built from the answers. For each: a name, a
one-line bio ("Dana, 34, runs a 3-person bakery, does her own Instagram"), what
they want, and what they would pay. Make them different on purpose: different
budgets, sizes, urgency and ways of buying.

## Step 3: the dates

Each islander speaks in their own voice in 2-3 lines: what they would say on a
first date with the user's offer. What excites them, what worries them, what
would make them walk away.

## Step 4: the recoupling

Score each islander 0-10 on 4 things, in a table:

- **Pain**: how badly do they need this?
- **Pay**: can and will they pay the price?
- **Reach**: can the user actually find and talk to them?
- **Fit**: would the user enjoy working with them (compare to the best and
  worst client)?

The bottom 6 are dumped from the villa, one line each. Of the top 3, the
villa votes and one couples up.

## Step 5: the dream client card

- Who they are, in one sentence.
- Their problem, in their words.
- What they have tried already.
- What makes them say yes, and what makes them say no.
- Where they hang out (types of places, not invented specific accounts).
- The first message to send one of them, which asks a question and does not
  sell.
- How to find 10 of them this week.

## Rules

- Personas are realistic types, not real named people.
- No invented statistics.
- No spam or mass cold outreach in the plan.
